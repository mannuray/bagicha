# Content standard

For a home gardener, not an academic: what to do, to this plant, in this
region, now.

**Rules**
- Concrete action with real months for the region. No filler ("monitor
  plant health", "maintain moisture consistency").
- Region-aware: write care for every region the plant is grown in. Two
  regions share only via an explicit `sameAs`, with the reason in a comment.
- Organic first, with amounts; chemical as the alternative with dose,
  "wear gloves" and waiting days before harvest. Only products approved for
  that crop in India; never banned ones.
- Doses scale by tree age (`upTo3y`, `y4to7`, `y8plus`) where it matters.
- Reminder title ≤ 60 characters, starts with a verb.
- "Only if you see …" sprays are `conditional`: plant page, never reminders.
- At most 3 reminders per tree per month reach the gardener; order the
  important ones by category (PROTECTION, FEEDING, PRUNING, WATERING, GENERAL).
- Say why in one short line.
- Unsure → "(check)" in the draft; resolve in review, never ship it.

**Good**
- "Hold back water until fruit sets" — Oct 15–Jan 31, bearing, North/East.
  Why: "A dry spell makes the tree flower."
- "Spray for hoppers as flowers appear" — organic "Neem oil 5 ml per litre";
  or "Imidacloprid 17.8 SL 0.3 ml per litre; wear gloves; never within 15
  days of picking".
- "Band the trunk against mealybug" — "Tie a 25 cm greased polythene band
  30 cm above ground."

**Bad**
- "Inspect new growth and branch health" (filler, any tree, any month).
- "Apply fertiliser as needed" (no what, how much or when).
- "Spray chlorpyriphos" (chemical first, no dose or safety, not approved).
