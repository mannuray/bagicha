Delete your Budtale account

You can delete your account yourself in the app, or ask us by email if you can't sign in.

In the app

  1. Open Budtale and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

If you can't sign in

Email [GRIEVANCE EMAIL] from the Google account's email address you used for Budtale, and ask us to delete the account. We reply within 30 days.

What happens next

  • Your account is deleted for good 30 days after you ask. During those 30 days your posts, replies and profile are hidden from others, and signing in lets you keep the account.
  • Everything you added is deleted: your profile, plants, journal, photos, posts, replies and messages.
  • We keep payment records (tax law, up to 8 years) and a security log (one year). Neither holds anything you wrote.

More detail is in the privacy policy.
