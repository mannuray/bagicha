# Litchi care: final for owner review (2026-11-12)

Sources (full links at the end): CIB&RC Major Uses of Pesticides to 30.09.2026 (MUP); ICAR-NRC Litchi Muzaffarpur web pages: pests (NRCL-P), diseases (NRCL-D), FAQ (NRCL-F), cultivars (NRCL-C); PAU Package of Practices for Fruit Crops (PAU); NHB litchi pages (NHB-n = lit00n.pdf); NIPHM/DPPQS AESA-based IPM package for litchi (NIPHM). NRCL's PDF host (nrclitchi.icar.gov.in) did not resolve, so its PDF bulletins were not read. Only its web pages were.

- Removed E row 5 (hold back water Oct–Jan, computed from "four months before flowering") and E row 10 (keep soil moist Mar 15–Jun 5, computed from fruit set and picking). The quoted advice moves to the plant page with no dates. Row 10 stays for N, whose dates PAU quotes.

- E Y1: the third window now follows NHB-7's wording ("after harvesting of fruits"), not "after the rains begin".
- Removed the E "don't water bearing trees" clause from the pre_flowering phase. It repeated removed row 5.
- "Wear gloves" was already on the only nutrient spray (boric acid). There are no pesticide lines.

Pending regions (keep today's advice): CENTRAL_INDIA, WEST_INDIA, SOUTH_DECCAN, COASTAL_HUMID (no litchi source with dates). HILLS_COOL is not listed as a growing region, as for mango.

**Label claims.** The MUP insecticide, fungicide, bio-insecticide and bio-fungicide lists have no claim of any kind for litchi. So this file has no chemical alternatives. Every NRCL, NIPHM and NHB spray is listed under "Removed".

Timing groups (no sameAs):
- **E = EAST_INDIA.** Bihar timing from NRCL: harvest mid-May to mid-June; fertiliser after harvest and at clove-size fruit.
- **N = NORTH_INDIA.** Punjab timing from PAU: harvest in the 2nd–3rd week of June. Manure in December, urea in February and April. One watering against frost in early December instead of holding water back.
- They differ by 3–4 weeks and follow different feeding and watering calendars, so they are not merged.
- E covers West Bengal, Jharkhand, Odisha and Assam with Bihar's dates; those states are not separately verified. N covers western UP (Saharanpur) with Punjab's dates.

Notes on dates:
- **E harvest (row 12)** comes from the NRCL cultivar dates: Purbi 3rd week of May; Mandraji last week of May to 1st week of June; Gandaki Lalima 2nd week of June; Gandaki Sampada mid-June. NHB-4 says Bihar picks "in early May"; I did not use that.
- E now has no watering reminder for bearing trees. NHB-6 and NRCL-F give the advice only relative to flowering, fruit set and picking. It is on the plant page.

**Dose** (rows 2, 3, 8, 9; Y1). Per year of age, up to 10 years (the cap age).
- **The rule.** ICAR institute first; where equally authoritative sources differ, the lower dose.
  - NRCL (ICAR) gives only a flat dose for trees over 12 years: 750 g N and 750 g K₂O a year (NRCL-F). It can't be put per year of age, so it is not the app's dose. It is given as text: on the plant page and in this note.
  - PAU p.74 gives age bands (1–3, 4–6, 7–10, above 10), not a per-year figure. The draft's straight line from PAU's over-10 dose ran up to 20% above those bands.
  - NHB-7 gives a planting-year dose plus a quoted "Annual Increase" for each year, to "mature trees". It is lower than the PAU line on N, P and FYM, so it is used.
- NHB-7's annual increase, converted (ammonium sulphate 20.6% N and potassium sulphate 50% K₂O; both factors are additions to the owner's list):
  - Ammonium sulphate 200 g × 0.206 = 41 g N → urea 41 ÷ 0.46 = **90 g** per year of age.
  - Superphosphate 100 g = **SSP 100 g** per year of age (16 g P₂O₅).
  - Potassium sulphate 100 g × 0.50 = 50 g K₂O → MOP 50 ÷ 0.60 = **83 g** per year of age.
  - FYM **4 kg** per year of age.
  - Cap at 10 years: urea 900 g, SSP 1000 g, MOP 830 g, FYM 40 kg a year.
- The per-age form drops NHB-7's planting-year base (FYM 10–20 kg; 200 g of each fertiliser), so it sits a little under NHB-7 at every age. At maturity NHB-7 gives 60 kg FYM and 2.2 kg ammonium sulphate (about 450 g N).

Splits:
- N (PAU's timing, p.74): December: FYM 4 kg + SSP 100 g + MOP 83 g × age. Mid-February: urea 45 g × age. Mid-April: urea 45 g × age.
- E (NRCL-F's split: ⅔ of N and K plus all P after harvest, ⅓ of N and K at clove size; FYM in September per NHB-7):
  - June: urea 60 g + SSP 100 g + MOP 55 g × age.
  - April: urea 30 g + MOP 28 g × age.
  - September: FYM 4 kg × age.
- Example, an 8-year-old: 32 kg FYM.
  - N: Dec SSP 800 g + MOP 665 g; Feb and Apr urea 360 g each.
  - E: Jun urea 480 g + SSP 800 g + MOP 440 g; Apr urea 240 g + MOP 225 g.

Owner to note:
- NHB-7's K (MOP 83 g × age, 500 g K₂O at the cap) is above PAU's (600 g MOP, 360 g K₂O, above 10 years). The rule picked NHB-7 as a whole because it is the only quoted per-year source. Taking PAU's K alone would be a mix of two sources.
- At the cap, N is about 414 g, against 736 g from PAU and 750 g from NRCL. NIPHM p.26 is higher still: 3.5 kg CAN, about 875 g N.

Plant page only (never reminders):
- Bearing-tree dose from NRCL, the ICAR institute: trees over 12 years, 750 g N and 750 g K₂O a year. This is a flat adult figure, not per year of age. The app's reminders use NHB-7's lower per-year dose. (NRCL-F)
- E, water before flowering: NHB-6 says to hold back water from bearing trees for about four months before flowering. Start watering again once flower bunches show. (NHB-6; NIPHM p.25)
- E, water as fruit grows: never let the soil dry from fruit set until 8–10 days before picking. (NRCL-F; NHB-6)
- Bagging: 25–30 days after fruit set, tie white or pink non-woven polypropylene bags over the bunches. They guard against nut borer, sunburn and cracking, and improve colour. (PAU p.75)
- N, if you see velvety curled leaves on the new flush (mite): cut and burn those shoots in Dec–Jan, before the flower buds open. (NIPHM p.26)
- If you see silk webs with chewed bark on the trunk (bark-eating caterpillar):
  - pull off the webs;
  - push a wire into the tunnel, or squirt in kerosene with a syringe;
  - seal the hole with mud.
  (NIPHM p.27; NHB-8; NRCL-P)
- If shoot tips wilt in Sep–Oct (shoot borer): clip and destroy them. (NIPHM p.27; NRCL-P)
- If leaves are rolled with caterpillars inside (leaf roller): pick off the rolled leaves. (NIPHM p.26–27; NRCL-P)
- N, if fruits have pin-holes at the stalk (nut borer): clear all fallen and dried fruit and dig the basin lightly after harvest. (PAU p.76)
- If beetles cut leaf edges (leaf-cutting weevil): shake the branches over an upturned umbrella and drop the beetles into soapy water. (NRCL-P)
- If fruits are misshapen (boron): spray boric acid 1 g per litre (0.1%). Wear gloves; it is a nutrient, so there is no waiting period. (NIPHM p.28)
- If leaves and twigs have dark sunken spots (anthracnose): collect and burn diseased leaves, twigs and fruit, and thin crowded branches. (NIPHM p.28)
- E, young trees need frequent watering in the dry months (NHB-6 gives no interval).
- Planting: don't plant where water stands; litchi wilt follows waterlogging. (NRCL-D)

## Phases
| Phase | E East | N North | Avoid | Watch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| harvest | mid-May–mid-Jun | Jun | Pulling single fruits; picking before fully red | Fruit borer, fruit rot |
| post_harvest_flush | Jun–Sep | Jul–Sep | Heavy pruning (light only; heavy is for old trees, NHB-5) | Mite (peaks Jul–Oct, NRCL-P), leaf roller in the rains, shoot borer (Sep–Oct) |
| pre_flowering | Oct–Jan | Oct–Jan | Deep digging outside Nov–Feb (NHB-5) | Mite on the new flush; mealybug nymphs climbing (Dec–Jan); N: frost |
| flowering | Feb–Mar | Feb–Mar | Spraying in full bloom (bees, NIPHM p.27); E: water stress at flowering, so start watering again once flower bunches show (NIPHM p.25) | Mite on flower bunches (Feb–Mar, NRCL-P), litchi bug |
| fruit_development | Mar–May | Apr–Jun | Dry soil then heavy watering (cracking, NHB-3) | Fruit borer (Apr–May, NRCL-P), cracking, sunburn (above 40 °C, PAU p.72) |
| young: establishment | all year | all year | Waterlogging; hot wind and frost on unprotected plants | Termites, wilt |

## Removed
- **Removed as unverified (final pass):**
  - row 11, "Bag the fruit bunches", E Apr 15–30 and N May 10–25: both computed from fruit set + PAU's "25–30 days after fruit set". The advice is on the plant page without dates;
  - E row 5, "Hold back water before flowering", Oct 1–Jan 31: computed from NHB-6's "four months before flowering";
  - E row 10, "Keep the soil moist until picking", Mar 15–Jun 5: computed from fruit set and picking;
  - E Y2, "Water the young tree", Oct 1–Jun 15: the frequency was PAU's (Punjab), borrowed for the East;
  - E Y1's "after the rains begin" wording (replaced by NHB-7's "after harvesting of fruits");
  - the straight-line dose from PAU's over-10 figure (up to 20% above PAU's bands; replaced by NHB-7).
- **Not approved (MUP 30.09.2026 has no litchi claim for any product):**
  - chlorfenapyr and propargite (mite, NRCL-P);
  - thiacloprid, imidacloprid, lufenuron, novaluron, cypermethrin and emamectin benzoate (fruit and shoot borer, NRCL-P);
  - spinosad and fipronil (leaf roller);
  - buprofezin, dimethoate, DDVP and chlorpyriphos (weevil, mealybug, bark caterpillar);
  - copper oxychloride, carbendazim, thiophanate methyl, chlorothalonil, difenoconazole, azoxystrobin and hexaconazole (NRCL-D);
  - dicofol or Kelthane (NIPHM p.26, NHB-8), which is not in the MUP at all;
  - neem oil 4 ml/L and panchagavya before flowering (NRCL-P; no azadirachtin claim for litchi);
  - diuron and atrazine weedkillers (NHB-5).
- **Not used:**
  - Planofix/NAA against fruit drop (NRCL-F), and 2,4-D, GA3 and Ethrel (NHB-3, NHB-4): I did not check the growth-regulator list;
  - boron "4 g per litre" in early April and early May (NRCL-F), because the product is not named and it is 4 times NIPHM's boric acid 0.1%. Only NIPHM's figure is kept, on the plant page.
- **Unverified from the draft:**
  - West Bengal, Jharkhand, Odisha and Assam timing as separate from Bihar;
  - NHB-4's "Bihar harvest in early May".
- **Not routine:** rejuvenating old trees (NRCL-F); air-layering and planting practice.
