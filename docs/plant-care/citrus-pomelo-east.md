# Citrus: pomelo kind, EAST_INDIA: second source search + source check (2026-11-24, for owner review)

This follows `docs/plant-care/citrus-pomelo.md`, which the owner approved for NORTH_INDIA and which leaves EAST_INDIA pending (its §5 and Q9). It records a second, wider search for an eastern pomelo source (batabi lebu, jambura, chakotra, robab tenga) and source-checks every candidate East row.

**Outcome: keep EAST_INDIA pending. Nothing is drafted for the seed.**
- **No eastern source gives pomelo care.** I looked for an extension guide, package of practices or KVK leaflet from Bengal, Assam, Bihar, Odisha or the North-East and found none. Every eastern pomelo paper found is germplasm or characterisation work.
- **The one new fact argues against lending the North calendar.** At BCKV's Mondouri station in Nadia, West Bengal, pummelo flowered between "first week of January to last week of February". The North row says Mar–Apr (IH 2026 p.19). So the North's dated rows can't be moved to the East unchanged.
- **The approved citrus doc has no EAST rows for any kind** (`citrus.md`, lines 285 and 336). So "reuse approved citrus rows" gives nothing for the East.
- **The Assam package (AAU 2023) has a "Plant Protection for Citrus" section.** It is written around Khasi mandarin and Assam lemon and never names pomelo. Its items are listed in §3 as candidates; all are removed on scope.

Rows drafted: **0 reminders, 0 plant-page items, 0 phases.** Candidates checked: 21 (§3); all were removed or held.
