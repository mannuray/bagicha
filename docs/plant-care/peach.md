# Peach care: draft + source check (2026-11-23)

Peach is a deciduous stone fruit. It fruits on **one-year-old shoots**, so it needs a hard prune every winter (PAU p.81; UHF p.34). Every calendar row below follows a month given by a regional guide:
- winter pruning and training;
- the December manure and the split urea;
- March fruit thinning in Punjab;
- dry-season watering;
- fruit-fly traps and picking.

Everything the gardener has to see first is `conditional` (plant page, never a reminder): aphids, leaf curl, gummosis, borers, beetles, scale, iron chlorosis and hand thinning in the hills.

Kinds: none. The catalogue has the plain entry (HILLS_COOL, NORTH_INDIA), Shan-i-Punjab, Florda Prince and Partap (NORTH_INDIA, PAU), and July Elberta (HILLS_COOL). `grownIn` = NORTH_INDIA, HILLS_COOL.

Sources (full links at the end):
- **PAU** = Package of Practices for Cultivation of Fruits. Peach is on printed pp.77–87. The Bordeaux paste and paint recipes are on p.10. Printed pages.
- **UHF** = *Cultivation practices for stone fruits & walnut*, by Dr YS Parmar UHF Nauni scientists for the HP Horticulture Development Project (World Bank), hosted by HP Horticulture (eudyan). Peach and nectarine are on pp.27–36; diseases on pp.62–66; pests on pp.68–69. It covers the mid hills (1000–2000 m). Printed pages.
- **HP-SS** = *Spray Schedule 2022 (Peach, Plum, Apricot & Almond)*, Dept of Horticulture HP with Dr YSPUHF Nauni. One page plus notes.
- **SKUAST** = SKUAST-Jammu, *Package of Practices for Fruit Crops* (2020), hosted by KVK Kishtwar. Peach is on pp.102–107. It covers the Jammu hills (Poonch, Doda, Kishtwar, Ramban, Udhampur, Reasi, Rajouri: HILLS_COOL) and the Jammu, Samba and Kathua plains, which the app maps to NORTH_INDIA. Printed pages.
- **HP Shiva** = *Package of Practices for Subtropical Fruit Crops of HP* (Dr YSPUHF, COHF Neri, 2022). It has no peach chapter. Its plum chapter is used here only for the cut-paste size (p.109).

## 2. Rules used

1. **Placement:** spread manure and fertiliser in the tree basin, about 30 cm from the trunk, and mix it in lightly (UHF p.26, in its cherry chapter). Water after. Wear gloves.
2. **Doses** go in `dose` only where the guide's table is an exact per-year rule. Banded tables are row text (citrus Q15, guava precedent).
3. **Bordeaux (owner exception, 2026-11-12):** paste on cuts and wounds. A spray only when no fruit is on the tree, and only where an ICAR or SAU guide names it (none does for peach: Q4).
   - **Bordeaux paste** (PAU p.10, scaled down 1:40):
     - Dissolve 50 g copper sulphate in 375 ml water.
     - In a second pot, slake 75 g quicklime slowly with a little of another 375 ml of water, then add the rest.
     - Mix the two and stir. Brush it on the cut.
     - Use a plastic or clay pot, never iron. Wear gloves.
   - **Bordeaux paint** (PAU p.10, scaled down 1:20) lasts about a year on a wound and keeps rain out:
     - Heat copper sulphate crystals (about 70 g) on an iron tawa until they crumble into a white powder (monohydrate). Weigh out 50 g of the powder.
     - Mix it well with 100 g hydrated lime dust, then stir in 150 ml boiled linseed oil until smooth.
     - Brush it on. It keeps in a closed pot. Wear gloves.
4. **Fertilisers and micronutrients** (urea, SSP, MOP, ferrous sulphate) have no waiting period.
5. **Chemicals:** only rows with a peach claim in MUP, with a per-litre dose and waiting days (§4). None survives.

### 3H. HILLS_COOL (UHF, SKUAST, HP-SS)

**Dose (H1, C12):** UHF p.35 gives N 70 g, P₂O₅ 35 g and K₂O 100 g per year of age, up to 7 years. The 7-year row reads 500 / 250 / 700 g, against 490 / 245 / 700 g from the rule (Q3). Converted per year of age:
- urea 70 ÷ 0.46 = **152 g**;
- SSP 35 ÷ 0.16 = **219 g**;
- MOP 100 ÷ 0.60 = **167 g**.

UHF's FYM is 10 kg at 1 year, then 5 kg more each year, to 40 kg at 7+. That is base + increment, so it goes in row text.

SKUAST's own table (p.104) is not used: FYM 10–25 kg and urea 55 / 115 / 170 / 225 / 280 / 395 / 510 / 620 / 735 g for years 1–9, which is per-year only to year 5. It is shown as a conflict.

**Removed lines with no replacement:**
- **Brown rot** (UHF): the only control given is captan before harvest and carbendazim at bloom, with no peach claim. No guide gives a non-chemical step, so there is no row. Picking ripe fruit on time (N8, C8) helps.
- **Powdery mildew, frosty mildew, leaf spot and rust** (UHF): mancozeb, wettable sulphur, carbendazim and thiophanate have no peach claim. Lime sulphur has a peach powdery-mildew claim, but no per-litre dose (§4).
- **Hairy caterpillar and plum caseworm** (PAU): PAU gives no control.
- **Girdling Shan-i-Punjab at full bloom** (PAU p.82): an orchard practice to advance maturity, not needed in a home garden.
- **Whitewashing the trunk** (SKUAST p.103, subtropical): no month, and PAU doesn't give it.
- **Bordeaux soil drench at the stem base:** no peach guide gives one. UHF and HP-SS drench with copper oxychloride or mancozeb for collar rot and white root rot (no peach claim), and use Bordeaux only as paint on the scraped collar (C11).

## 6. Open questions for the owner
- **Q1. PAU's dose is banded** (urea is 1000 g at both 4 and 5+ years; MOP is 830 g at 4). Rows N1, N3 and N4 carry the bands as text, with no per-year `dose`.
  - Recommendation: **accept the text**, as for citrus Q15. A fitted rule (e.g. urea 180 g × age) would match years 1–3 and miss year 4, which would be inventing.
- **Q2. HILLS_COOL spans 400 m to 3000 m.** UHF's peach belt is 1000–2000 m, and low-chill kinds grow in the low hills. Bloom, picking and the urea split shift by weeks with altitude.
  - Recommendation: **ship the four month-based rows** (Dec manure, Jan pruning, Apr–Jun water, Apr–Aug traps). Each is stated by a hills source and is broad enough for both belts. Keep the bloom-tied urea (C12) and picking on the plant page.
- **Q3. UHF's 7-year cap** reads 500 g N and 250 g P₂O₅, against 490 g and 245 g from the per-year rule.
  - Recommendation: **use the per-year rule** (152 g urea, 219 g SSP and 167 g MOP × age, maxAge 7). The gap is 2%.
- **Q4. A dormant 1% Bordeaux spray for leaf curl.**
  - UHF (January, late dormancy), SKUAST and HP-SS (white bud) all time a copper spray before bud swell, but they name copper oxychloride, which has no peach claim.
  - Only the Meghalaya agriculture department names "1% Bordeaux mixture before bud swells". It is a state department, not an ICAR or SAU guide, so it is outside the exception as written.
  - PAU's own spray recipe is Bordeaux 2:2:250 (p.11): 2 kg copper sulphate + 2 kg quicklime in 250 L, which is 0.8% (8 g + 8 g per litre), each dissolved or slaked separately in non-metal pots, then strained and mixed and tested with a bright nail. The approved pomegranate 1% (NRC) is 10 g copper sulphate + 3–4 g quicklime per litre.
  - Recommendation: **leave the spray out**, since the guide test isn't met. C3 stays sanitation only. If the owner widens the exception, add a January conditional for HILLS (a tree that had leaf curl last year; no fruit on the tree) using PAU's 2:2:250 recipe above, with gloves.
- **Q5. Oxydemeton-methyl 25 EC for peach leaf curl aphid:** labelled (1 ml/L), but MUP gives no waiting period. It is sprayed at pink bud, about three months before picking. It is also highly toxic (WHO class Ib).
  - Recommendation: **leave it out** (guava precedent: no waiting period, no row). The water jet and snipping in C1 are enough for a home tree.
- **Q6. Chilling or dormancy breakers** (hydrogen cyanamide) and thinning sprays (ethephon) have no peach claim. Hydrogen cyanamide is labelled for grapes only.
  - Recommendation: **leave them out**. Low-chill varieties (Partap, Florda Prince, Shan-i-Punjab) are chosen to need none in Punjab (PAU p.77: under 300 chilling hours).
- **Q7. Bordeaux paint** (PAU) needs copper sulphate heated on a tawa.
  - Recommendation: **keep it as the optional second coat** in N2 and for collar rot (C11), with the recipe and gloves. Paste alone is acceptable if the owner prefers a simpler step.
