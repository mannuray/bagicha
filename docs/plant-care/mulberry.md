# Mulberry (shahtoot) care: draft + source check (2026-11-24)

**Verdict: keep mulberry in `CARE_PENDING`.** No ICAR institute or state agricultural university package of practices for **fruit** mulberry was found for any of the five regions. Every guide that gives months, doses or pruning for mulberry in India is a **sericulture (leaf) guide**: a bush cut to stumps several times a year and fed heavily for leaf. Those steps would harm a fruiting tree, so none of them are used as calendar care.

This draft therefore has:
- **no calendar rows** in any region (no source ties a fruit-tree step to a month);
- **no per-year `dose`** (no fruit-tree dose was found);
- **no phases** (no source gives a fruit-tree season by month);
- seven **conditional items, drafted and held**. Each comes from a source whose step applies to a fruiting tree; the reason is given per row. They ship only if a region ships, or if the engine shows conditionals for a pending region (Q2).

Kinds: none. The catalogue has the plain entry (NORTH_INDIA, EAST_INDIA, HILLS_COOL, SOUTH_DECCAN), Black (NORTH_INDIA, HILLS_COOL) and Long (NORTH_INDIA, WEST_INDIA). `grownIn` = NORTH_INDIA, EAST_INDIA, HILLS_COOL, SOUTH_DECCAN, WEST_INDIA. All five go in `pendingRegions`.

Sources (full links at the end):
- **CIAH-IH** = Gurjar, Sarolia, Berwal and Surajbhan, *Post-harvest handling, packaging and value addition protocols for mulberry*, Indian Horticulture, May–June 2024, pp.25–27 (ICAR-CIAH Bikaner). This is the only fruit-mulberry source found. It covers the CIAH varieties Thar Harit (*Morus alba*) and Thar Lohit (*Morus nigra*), harvest and handling. It gives no months, doses or pruning. Printed pages.
- **CIAH-68** = Maheshwari and Haldhar, *Disease management in arid horticultural crops*, CIAH Tech. Bull. 68 (2018). Mulberry is on printed pp.19–20, treated as an arid **fruit** crop ("the ripe fruit is highly appreciated…"). Wayback copy (the CIAH host does not resolve).
- **CIAH-IPM** = Haldhar and Maheshwari, *Insect-pests management in arid and semi-arid horticultural crops* (CIAH, 2018). Mulberry appears only in the host list of the anar butterfly (p.14). No mulberry step. Wayback copy.
- **PAU** = Package of Practices for Cultivation of Fruits. It has **no mulberry chapter**. Mulberry appears only as a windbreak tree (planting chapter), as basket material for grapes (p.94), and among fruits that squirrels damage (p.156). Printed pages.
- **TNAU-S** = Crop Production Guide, Sericulture (Directorate of Agriculture TN with TNAU), chapter 14, printed pp.445–453 (pests and diseases p.448). Mulberry for **silkworm leaf**. Only its mechanical pest steps are used, and only where they apply to a fruiting tree (§3).
- **MUP** = CIB&RC Major Uses of Pesticides to 30.09.2026: insecticides, fungicides, bio-insecticides, bio-fungicides, herbicides and PGR lists, all searched for "mulberry" and "Morus".

## 2. Rules used

1. **No sericulture practice as calendar care.** Bottom pruning to 10 cm, pruning to 30–35 cm, five prunings a year, fertiliser "after every pruning or leaf harvest", 20 t/ha FYM and 300:120:120 kg NPK/ha are all for a leaf bush (TNAU-S pp.445–447). They are listed in §5 as removed.
2. **A sericulture step is kept only if it works the same on a fruiting tree**, such as cutting out an infested shoot or scraping off scale. The reason is written in each row.
3. **Chemicals:** only rows with a mulberry claim in MUP. There are none for pests or diseases (§4), so every row is organic or mechanical.
4. **Neem products and biological agents dropped** (citrus, papaya and pomegranate precedent): no mulberry claim.
5. **Bordeaux:** the owner exception needs an ICAR or SAU guide that recommends it for this crop. None was found, so there is no Bordeaux step (Q3).

That is **7 held conditionals** (H1–H7). H3–H6 are from a leaf-bush guide for Tamil Nadu and are offered for review, not for shipping on their own.

**Removed lines with no replacement:**
- **Ants that tend mealybug:** TNAU-S's only step is endosulfan or malathion dust. Endosulfan is banned; malathion has no mulberry claim.
- **Leaf webber timing:** TNAU-S's "irrigate immediately after pruning", parasitoid releases "next day" and "10 days after pruning", and the spray "30 days after pruning" are timed from the leaf-bush pruning cycle.
- **Root rot extras:** neem cake 1 t/ha in five splits (field rate for a leaf plantation), copper oxychloride 2 g/L (no mulberry claim), and *Pseudomonas* + *Trichoderma* + FYM 100 g per plant (biologicals, no claim).
- **Anar butterfly:** CIAH-IPM lists mulberry as a host but gives no mulberry step. Not added.

## 5. What the check changed

1. **All sericulture calendar care removed:** bottom pruning to 10 cm once a year, other prunings at 30–35 cm, five prunings a year; FYM 20 t/ha/yr; 300:120:120 kg NPK/ha "after every pruning"; fertiliser "3 weeks after every pruning or leaf harvest"; June/November pruning (pit system). All are for a leaf bush. The untraced "June–July pruning after fruit harvest, Eastern India" snippet is not used either (Q6).
2. **The GA PGR claim not used** (leaf yield, §4).
3. **No winter pruning month**, although foreign and nursery pages say "prune in winter dormancy": no Indian ICAR or SAU source says so for fruit mulberry in any region.
4. **No feed dose:** the one Indian fruit figure seen (a "50-50-50 g per plant" for bearing black mulberry in a ResearchGate book chapter) was not opened, and a chapter is not a package of practices.
5. **Every chemical dropped** (§4). **Neem and biologicals dropped** (precedent).
6. **No Bordeaux step** (Q3).
7. **All five regions pending.**

## 6. Open questions for the owner
- **Q1. Keep mulberry pending?** No region has a fruit-mulberry package of practices; every Indian guide with months and doses is for silkworm leaf. Recommendation: **yes, keep `mulberry` in `CARE_PENDING`** and leave all five regions on the season pack.
- **Q2. Show the held conditionals?** H1–H7 are ready, but they are all the care there would be. Recommendation: **don't ship a mulberry record for conditionals alone.** If the engine later shows conditionals for pending regions, add H1, H2 and H7 (fruit sources) first; add H3–H6 (leaf-bush guide, Tamil Nadu) only if you accept rule 2.2.
- **Q3. Bordeaux paste on cuts** without a mulberry-specific ICAR or SAU citation? Recommendation: **no.** The exception is tied to a guide recommending it for the crop; revisit when a fruit-mulberry guide is found.
- **Q4. Ask CIAH Bikaner (or the Bikaner KVK) for its fruit-mulberry production technology.** CIAH-IH says CIAH has developed "production technology for commercial cultivation of mulberry under hot arid and semi-arid areas", but no bulletin was found online. Recommendation: **yes.** It is the likeliest path to a WEST_INDIA calendar (pruning month, dose, season for Thar Lohit). A PAU or SKUAST fruit chapter in a later edition would unlock NORTH or HILLS.
- **Q5. Pruning advice in the meantime?** Foreign guides say mulberry needs only light winter pruning of dead and crossing wood. Recommendation: **leave it out** until an Indian fruit source says so.
- **Q6. EAST_INDIA lead:** an untraced snippet says fruit mulberry is pruned after fruit harvest in June–July in Eastern India. Recommendation: **ask BAU Sabour or a Bihar/West Bengal KVK** whether they have a fruit-mulberry advisory; use nothing until one is found.
