| 11 | Pick fruit with a stalk (NEW) | Jun 10–Aug 15 | May 1–Jun 30 | May 1–Jun 30 | Apr 1–Jul 15 | Mar 15–May 31 | Pick with a 10–20 cm stalk; keep fruit stalk-down to drain the sap | — | Sap burns the skin and spoils fruit | NHB-4; PAU |

Dose (rows 2–3, and Y1): per year of age, up to 10 years — FYM 5 kg once a year; chemical per dose (two doses) urea 110 g + SSP 155 g + MOP 85 g × age. (A 6-year-old: 30 kg FYM; 660 g urea + 930 g SSP + 510 g MOP per dose.) Source: NHB-7.

Plant page only (never reminders):
- If hoppers are still more than 5 per flower stalk at pea-size fruit: repeat row 6. (NHM; NHB-8)
- If you see holes with sawdust on the trunk (stem borer): hook out the grub with wire, plug the hole with cotton soaked in a little kerosene, seal with mud; cut and burn badly bored branches. (PAU; NHB-8)
- If flower bunches are bunched and malformed: cut them off with 15–20 cm of healthy wood and burn. (POP §9.3; NHM)
- D, humid weather at flowering: azoxystrobin 23 SC 1 ml per litre against blossom blight; wear gloves; not within 5 days of picking. (MUP-F p.2)

| Phase | A | Central/West | C | D | Avoid | Watch |
