# Ber care: draft + source check (2026-11-24)

Ber is the Indian jujube, *Ziziphus mauritiana*. Every guide read gives it the same yearly cycle:
- the tree is pruned hard once a year after harvest;
- it rests, often leafless, through the hot dry summer;
- it grows with the rains, flowers after the monsoon, and ripens its fruit in winter and spring.

Most of that follows the calendar, so a region with a source gets dated reminders. Pests and diseases the gardener has to see first are `conditional` (plant page only).

Kinds: none. The catalogue has the plain entry (NORTH, WEST, CENTRAL, SOUTH_DECCAN), Gola and Umran (NORTH, WEST), Banarasi Karaka, and Apple Ber (every region). `grownIn` = NORTH_INDIA, WEST_INDIA, CENTRAL_INDIA, SOUTH_DECCAN, EAST_INDIA, COASTAL_HUMID.

**Apple Ber.** Only the Assam package (AAU) names it: its cultivars are "Apple, BAU, Thailand". Its hard heading-back to 30 cm above the graft union is the Apple Ber way of pruning. PAU, TNAU, CAZRI, NIPHM and NHB do not mention Apple Ber. It is the same species, so this draft gives it the family's care with no kind of its own (Q9).

There is no `sameAs`. The four sourced regions differ in pruning month (NORTH May 16–31, WEST May, SOUTH Feb–Mar, EAST after a Jan–Feb harvest) and in harvest season.

## 2. Rules used

1. **Placement of manure and fertiliser** (NIPHM p.23): in a trench 20–30 cm deep and 30 cm wide along the drip line of the tree. Wear gloves for fertilisers.
2. **Doses per year of age, as the guides give them.** Only PAU gives a per-year rule. Banded and flat tables are shown and never turned into a per-year rule.
3. **Bordeaux (owner exception, 2026-11-12).**
   - **Bordeaux paste on cuts** (PAU p.10, "Apply the paste … to the cut ends with a brush"). PAU's batch is 2 kg copper sulphate dissolved in 15 L water, and 3 kg quicklime slaked in another 15 L. **Scaled down by me to 1/20:**
     - dissolve 100 g copper sulphate in 750 ml water in a plastic or clay pot, never metal (PAU p.11);
     - slake 150 g quicklime slowly in a little of another 750 ml water, then add the rest;
     - mix the two and stir. That makes about 1.5 L of paste. Brush it on cuts the day it is made. Wear gloves.
   - **No 1% Bordeaux spray and no stem-base drench.** No ber guide read recommends either (Q8).
   - NIPHM (p.22) pastes die-back cuts with **cow dung**. It is offered as the simple alternative in C9.
4. **Fertilisers, potassium nitrate and borax** have no waiting period.
5. **Chemicals:** only rows with a ber claim in MUP and a waiting period on the label. **None qualifies** (§4). Every pest and disease line is cultural or mechanical.
6. **Neem products dropped** (precedent): TNAU's neem oil 30 ml/L, NIPHM's azadirachtin 1%, neem powder and NSKE 5% for birds. Ber has no neem claim.

## 6. Open questions for the owner
- **Q1. Carbendazim for powdery mildew.** It is the only usable ber label claim: carbendazim 50% WP, 1 g per litre (10 g in 10 L per tree). The label gives no waiting days. Mildew strikes flowers and young fruit, so a "no fruit on the tree" limit (the apple lime-sulphur route) never applies. Recommendation: **hold it**, following cherry Q6. Mildew stays cultural (C2). Revisit if a label with waiting days appears.
- **Q2. WEST feed dose.** CAZRI's table is banded and not linear: FYM 10/15/20/25/30 kg, urea 220/440/660/1100/1300 g, SSP 300–1500 g, MOP 80–400 g at 1–5+ years. It is given with all the FYM, SSP and MOP plus half the urea in July, and the other half of the urea in November after fruit set. Recommendation: **hold W5 and W7.** Don't borrow PAU's per-year rule for Rajasthan, and don't fit a line to CAZRI's table.
- **Q3. Flat bearing feeds (SOUTH, EAST).** TNAU gives 50 kg FYM + N 500 g, P 200 g, K 500 g from the 2nd year on, right after pruning. AAU gives 10–12 kg FYM + N 200 g, P₂O₅ 250 g, K₂O 100 g from the 2nd year on, in March and October with no split, and 25 g borax with each. Recommendation:
  - ship the **manure** as flat text for bearing trees: S2 50 kg, E2 10 kg + 25 g borax;
  - **hold the fertiliser parts.** TNAU's P and K may be elements or oxides, and AAU gives no split. Shipping them would mean my own urea/SSP/MOP arithmetic or a 50:50 split;
  - young trees in SOUTH and EAST get no calendar feed.
- **Q4. WEST follows Rajasthan?** CAZRI (Jodhpur) gives May pruning. Search summaries (not read) say Gujarat and Maharashtra finish pruning by end-April, and a South Gujarat trial favoured the second half of April for Gola. NHB puts the Gujarat harvest in Dec–Mar against Jan–Mar for Rajasthan. Recommendation: **ship WEST on CAZRI** (W1–W4, W6 + conditionals), as NORTH follows Punjab. Re-check when an MPKV, Dr PDKV or Gujarat SAU package is found. If the owner prefers the pomegranate-WEST caution, keep it in `pendingRegions`.
- **Q5. NORTH second urea split in October.** PAU says "soon after the fruit set" and mulches "in the month of October after application of second split". Recommendation: **accept October** (N4).
- **Q6. SOUTH = Tamil Nadu.** TNAU prunes in Feb–Mar after an Oct–Nov harvest. A Karnataka (Bagalkot) cultivar trial pruned in the second week of May. That is a trial, not a package, but it hints the Deccan plateau may run on a North-like cycle. Recommendation: **ship SOUTH on TNAU** (pomegranate Q8 precedent) and re-check when a UAS or UHS package is found.
- **Q7. EAST = Assam, with a derived March pruning.** AAU is the only EAST source and the only Apple Ber source. Bihar and West Bengal are drier and may prune and harvest at other times. Recommendation: **ship EAST** with E1 in March, as derived from AAU's Jan–Feb harvest and "March (after pruning)". Re-check with a BAU Sabour or BCKV source.
- **Q8. Bordeaux 1% spray and stem-base drench.** The owner exception allows them, but no ber guide read recommends either: PAU's ber chapter has no Bordeaux line, and NIPHM uses cow-dung paste. Recommendation: **paste on cuts only** (PAU's general recipe). Add a spray only if a ber source is found.
- **Q9. Apple Ber as a kind?** Only AAU covers it. Its pruning (back to 30 cm above the graft) is harsher than PAU's 8 buds or CAZRI's 4th–6th side shoot. Recommendation: **no kind for now.** An Apple Ber in NORTH or WEST follows that region's pruning. Revisit if an Apple Ber guide for North or West India is found.
- **Q10. Mulch amount.** PAU gives paddy straw at 5 t per acre (about 1.2 kg per square metre), with no depth. The draft says "a thick layer". Recommendation: **keep "a thick layer"** and the PAU figure; don't add a depth no guide gives.
- **Q11. Methyl eugenol traps for fruit fly** (TNAU 25 per ha, AAU 20 per ha, NIPHM). Methyl eugenol draws males of *Bactrocera* fruit flies. No source read shows it draws the ber fly *Carpomyia vesuviana*, and the trap recipes add malathion or dichlorvos (no claim, or banned). Recommendation: **leave the traps out.**
