# Pomegranate care, WEST_INDIA: second search, draft and source check (2026-11-24)

This doc follows up Q3 in `pomegranate.md`. The owner decided there is **no bahar**: the tree flowers naturally. This search looked for a Maharashtra, Gujarat or Rajasthan source that gives either of these:
- calendar care for a naturally flowering or home-garden pomegranate;
- a dose per year of the tree's age.

The rules are the same as `pomegranate.md`:
- content standard;
- rule 2.2: banded tables are never turned into a per-year rule;
- chemicals only with a CIB&RC pomegranate claim (§4).

**Verdict: nothing usable was found. Keep WEST_INDIA in `pendingRegions`.**
- Every West source that gives a month or a dose either times it from a bahar, or gives banded or flat amounts that disagree with each other.
- One more source, ICAR-CAZRI Jodhpur, says an unregulated tree "bears flower and fruits throughout the year". That makes four sources with no natural-tree calendar (NHB pom005, TNAU p.36, CIAH p.8, CAZRI).
- **New calendar rows drafted: 0.** The three draft young-tree feed rows from `pomegranate.md` §3W (W1–W3) were re-checked. They stay held, and one of their notes is corrected.

## Open questions for the owner

- **Q-W1. Keep WEST_INDIA pending?**
  - No West source gives a natural-flowering calendar.
  - Young-tree months still conflict: Maharashtra (NHB, Govt of MH) Jul/Sep/Feb against Rajasthan (CIAH) Jan/Jun/Sep. Only September is shared.
  - **Recommendation: keep pending.** If the engine shows conditionals for a pending region, ship C1–C11 there. Otherwise WEST gardeners get nothing until a source appears.
- **Q-W2. Accept a dose from a table?**
  - CIAH Table 2's FYM column is 10 kg × age, up to 40 kg at age 4.
  - NHB (Maharashtra) gives 5/10/20/20/30–40 kg at ages 2/3/4/5/5+.
  - CAZRI gives a flat 15 kg in the monsoon.
  - **Recommendation: hold.** Reading CIAH's column as a per-year rule breaks rule 2.2, and the three West sources disagree by up to 4×. Revisit only with an MPKV or NRC package that states a per-year rule.
- **Q-W3. Add "never prune in the rains" to C10 for every region?** (NRC FAQ Q37.) **Recommendation: yes, as text only.** It is a sourced caution with no date and suits a novice. It changes no region's rows. It needs its own small review, since C10 is already approved.
- **Q-W4. Split WEST into Maharashtra and Rajasthan behaviour?** The region joins a semi-arid black-soil state with a hot arid one, and their young-tree months differ. **Recommendation: no.** The region model is a product decision outside this plant. Note it for the region review instead.
- **Q-W5. Where to look next.** **Recommendation:**
  - ask NRC Solapur, via its contact address or a KVK, for its package of practices or a home-garden note;
  - fetch MPKV's Marathi *Krishi Darshani* pomegranate chapter in a readable copy;
  - retry Wayback for NRC advisories 149 and 151 and Publication/20–52 when the service is back.
