# Flowers Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Give flowers their own treatment:
- seasonal flowers on the crop calendar and journey;
- perennial flowers on the tree page with flower stages, bloom months and a curated care calendar;
- flowers on Home and the Plants tab.

**Architecture:**
- **Seasonal flowers:** calendar entries with `kind: 'FLOWER'`, created as FLOWER records with a `crop_id`, so they ride every crop-journey branch.
- **Perennial flowers:** FLOWER records without a `crop_id`. A new `flower-care.ts` domain file holds species bloom months, care-by-month and life ranges. The per-plant materialize path uses it instead of the fruit pack, with a new `TaskSource.FLOWER_CARE`.
- **Web:** reuses the crops screens and the biography page, switching wording on `kind` / `record_kind`.

**Tech Stack:** NestJS 11, Prisma 6 (Postgres), jest and supertest; React 19, TanStack Query 5, zod 4, react-i18next, Vitest + MSW, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-flowers-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- **Branch:** `backend-cleanup`. Commits end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- **Migrations:** generated with `prisma migrate diff`, applied with `migrate deploy` and then `prisma generate`. Never reset.
- **No fake data:** bloom months, care months and life ranges are typical ranges, shown as "usually" or "about". No weather, no health scores.
- **Seasonal flowers** are never offered in the add form, and the rotation note never shows for flowers.
- **The fruit pack** (`SEASONAL_PACK`, `PERSONALIZED_OVERLAY`) never runs on a FLOWER record.
- **Copy** lives in `web/src/locales/en.json`. The Devanagari alias is the first alias matching `/[ऀ-ॿ]/`.
- **Checks:**
  - web: `npx vitest run`, `npx tsc -b`, `npm run lint` (baseline 15 warnings), `npm run check:boundaries`, `npm run check:cycles`;
  - backend: `npm run lint`, `npx jest`, `npx jest --config test/jest-e2e.json --forceExit`, `node scripts/check-module-imports.js`.

**Interfaces:**
- **Produces:**
  - `CropInfo.kind: 'CROP' | 'FLOWER'`, defaulting to `'CROP'` for the 26 vegetables.
  - `FLOWERS: CropInfo[]` (16 entries, ids below).
  - `CROPS` is the vegetables followed by `FLOWERS`, so `cropById` finds flowers.
  - The calendar and detail items gain `kind`.
  - Flower tags may include `'long'`: `harvest_span_days >= 60` means long blooming.
  - `PLANT_LIST_KINDS = ['tree', 'crop', 'flower']`:
    - `tree` means `record_kind = TREE` and `crop_id = null`;
    - `crop` means `crop_id != null` and `record_kind != FLOWER`;
    - `flower` means `record_kind = FLOWER`.
- **Flower ids (16, all from the spec):** marigold, zinnia, cosmos, petunia, calendula, dianthus, sunflower, balsam, portulaca, aster, salvia, celosia, gomphrena, sweet_pea, pansy, larkspur. The spec's African and French marigold are one `marigold` entry.
- **Flower windows** are typical Indian sowing months per climate group:
  - winter flowers (calendula, dianthus, petunia, pansy, sweet pea, larkspur, aster): PLAINS Sep–Nov, PENINSULAR Oct–Dec, HILLS Mar–May;
  - summer and monsoon flowers (zinnia, cosmos, balsam, portulaca, celosia, gomphrena, sunflower): PLAINS Feb–Jul, PENINSULAR Jan–Aug, HILLS Apr–Jun;
  - marigold: PLAINS Jun–Jul and Sep–Oct, PENINSULAR Jun–Oct, HILLS Apr–May;
  - salvia: PLAINS Sep–Oct and Feb–Mar.
- **Care keys:** `pinch` (after TRANSPLANTING, or after SOWING for direct crops, 20–25 days) for marigold, zinnia, cosmos, petunia and salvia; `deadhead` (after the first bloom: after TRANSPLANTING or SOWING at `days_to_first_harvest[0]`) for every flower; `stake` for sunflower.
- **New `CropFamily` values:** PORTULACACEAE, BALSAMINACEAE, CARYOPHYLLACEAE, LAMIACEAE, VIOLACEAE, RANUNCULACEAE. ASTERACEAE, SOLANACEAE, AMARANTHACEAE and FABACEAE are reused.

**Interfaces:**
- **Produces:**
  - `PERENNIAL_FLOWERS: PerennialFlower[]`, with `PerennialFlower = { family: string; name: string; aliases: string[]; bloom: Record<ClimateGroup, Months[]>; care: Record<ClimateGroup, Array<{ key: string; month: number; title: string; category: 'PRUNING'|'FEEDING'|'GENERAL' }>>; first_bloom_months: [number, number]; lifespan_months: [number, number] }`.
  - `perennialFlower(family: string): PerennialFlower | undefined`.
  - `flowerCareDue(flower, group, now): Array<{ key; title; category; due: Date; sourceKey: string }>`:
    - it returns care items whose month is the current or the next IST month;
    - `due` is the later of now and the 1st of that month (IST);
    - `sourceKey` is `${family}:${key}:${year}`.
  - `bloomStatus(flower, group, now): { status: 'in_season' | 'later'; window_label: string }` (e.g. "Mar–Jun").
  - `SpeciesNormalizationService.resolveFlowerFamily(input): string | null`.
  - The plant detail gains `bloom: { status, window_label } | null`. It's non-null only for a FLOWER record without a `crop_id` whose family is known.
  - `TaskSource.FLOWER_CARE`.
- **Families and aliases:**

  | Family | Aliases | First bloom | Lifespan |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | rose | rose, gulab, गुलाब, china rose | 4–8 months | 10–15 years |
  | hibiscus | hibiscus, gudhal, गुड़हल, jaswand, shoe flower | 6–12 months | 10–20 years |
  | jasmine | jasmine, mogra, मोगरा, chameli, juhi | 6–12 months | 10–20 years |
  | bougainvillea | bougainvillea, बोगनविलिया | 8–18 months | 20–30 years |
  | ixora | ixora, rugmini | 6–12 months | 10–20 years |
  | oleander | oleander, kaner, कनेर | 12–24 months | 20–40 years |
  | parijat | parijat, harsingar, हरसिंगार, night jasmine | 24–36 months | 20–40 years |
  | plumeria | plumeria, champa, चंपा, frangipani | 24–36 months | 30–50 years |
  | adenium | adenium, desert rose | 12–24 months | 15–25 years |
  | periwinkle | periwinkle, sadabahar, सदाबहार, vinca | 2–3 months | 2–3 years |

  Bloom months and care months (PLAINS; PENINSULAR and HILLS adjusted) are typical values:
  - **rose:** blooms Nov–Mar; hard prune in Oct; feed in Nov and Feb.
  - **hibiscus:** blooms Mar–Oct; prune in Feb; feed in Mar, May and Jul.
  - **mogra:** blooms Mar–Jun; prune in Dec; feed in Feb.
  - **bougainvillea:** blooms Feb–May and Oct–Nov; prune in Jun and Dec.
  - **ixora:** blooms Apr–Oct; prune in Feb.
  - **kaner:** blooms Apr–Oct; prune in Feb.
  - **parijat:** blooms Aug–Nov; prune in Feb.
  - **champa:** blooms Apr–Sep; prune in Jan.
  - **adenium:** blooms Mar–Jun and Oct–Nov; prune in Feb.
  - **sadabahar:** blooms all year; pinch in Mar.
- **Matching:** `resolveFlowerFamily` is checked **before** the fruit families when `record_kind === FLOWER`. Remove `'rose scented'` from the litchi aliases.
- **Lifecycle for FLOWER records without a crop:** below `first_bloom_months[0]` the stage is `JUVENILE`; after that, or once `first_flowering_at` is set, it's `FIRST_FLOWERING`. It is never `FIRST_HARVEST` or `MATURE_BEARING`. An unknown family uses 6–12 months.
- **Life profiles:** add the 10 families to `species-life-profiles.ts`, with `first_fruit_months` holding the first-bloom range and `productive_until_months` equal to the lifespan. Add `note_key: 'shortLived'` for periwinkle.
- **Catalogue:** add one entry per family to the bootstrap list in `plants.service.ts`, with `plant_type: 'perennial_flower'` and `species_family` equal to the family.
